The artist behind the work

Jenny Pearl

Born in Mansfield, Ohio, and raised in Florida from the early 1980s onward, Jennifer Kay Pearl has spent more than three decades working as a professional artist, muralist, designer, and creative entrepreneur in the Tampa Bay region and beyond.

Her experience spans public and private murals, trompe l’oeil, faux finishes, fine art, holiday window painting, signage, furniture, mixed media, photography, poetry, graphic design, and web-based creative projects. Her work has appeared in homes, businesses, community settings, public art projects, and commissioned environments.

My art is an act of sanctuary—a way to honor beauty, memory, spirit, and the shared places where people can feel connected again.
