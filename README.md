## Welcome

**My Art Gallery** is the official online portfolio of **Artist Jenny Pearl**, showcasing more than three decades of professional artistic work including public murals, fine art, decorative painting, photography, poetry, mixed media, digital design, and creative community projects.

This repository serves as the public landing page for the **My Art Gallery** website and is part of the larger **Verve N Veda™** creative ecosystem.

Rather than functioning as a traditional gallery, this project is designed to become a living archive documenting an artistic career while continuing to grow with new work, exhibitions, commissions, and community collaborations.

---

# Artistic Philosophy

My work is rooted in the belief that art has the ability to reconnect people to one another, to nature, and to themselves.

Whether creating a mural that transforms a public space, a watercolor inspired by Florida's landscapes, or a quiet poem reflecting on everyday beauty, every piece is created with the intention of leaving the world a little more hopeful than I found it.

Art is both memory and possibility.
